Terms & Conditions — child safety and GPS tracking

  1. Purpose: This system exists only to show the live location of the school bus, helping keep children safe and on time.
  2. Driver consent: The driver’s GPS location is shared only when the driver taps "Start sharing" to begin. Keep sharing on only during school duty hours and switch it off afterwards.
  3. Who can see what: Parents can see only the location of their own child’s bus. The school manager can see all buses. No third party gets access.
  4. Data use: Location data is used only for bus tracking. It is never sold or used for advertising. Only the latest bus location is stored; no history is kept.
  5. Login security: Do not share your Google account. Login is possible only with an email added by the school.
  6. No misuse: Sharing screenshots or bus location details on social media or with outsiders is prohibited. Access may be suspended for violations.
  7. While driving: Drivers must not use the phone while driving. Keep it in a holder and press buttons only when the vehicle is stopped.
  8. Accuracy: GPS Location may show delays or small differences because of network and weather. Treat it as approximate.
  9. Emergencies: In an emergency parents should contact the school or driver directly and not rely only on this app.
  10. Platform fee: A small monthly platform fee (set by the school) applies per registered student. It covers the tracking service only; no school or tuition fee is handled here.
  11. Changes: The school may update these terms from time to time. Continuing to log in means you accept the new terms.
